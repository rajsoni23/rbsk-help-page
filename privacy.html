Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SHREE RBSK Automation collects, uses, and protects information when you use the extension.

1. Purpose

SHREE RBSK Automation is designed to automate Student Registration and Child Screening data entry on the official RBSK portal. The extension works only to assist authorized users with faster and more accurate form filling.

2. Information We Collect

The extension may collect or store the following information:

  • Email address used for authentication.
  • Extension settings and preferences.
  • Automation configuration.
  • Device identifier (when required for license validation).

The extension does not collect passwords, payment information, bank details, browsing history, personal communications, or location data.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Authenticate authorized users.
  • Provide access to extension features.
  • Save user preferences.
  • Improve extension reliability and security.
  • Prevent unauthorized use.

4. Medical Data

The extension automates data entry based only on information supplied by the authorized user. It does not permanently store patient or medical information outside the official RBSK system.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except when required by law or to provide authentication services.

6. Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect user information. Authentication is performed using secure services, and extension resources are packaged within the extension.

7. Third-Party Services

The extension may use trusted third-party services such as Firebase for authentication and secure data storage.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request information regarding your stored account information or to request its removal where applicable.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
